# Questions people ask

**Is $10K a month guaranteed?** No. It's the destination on the map. What you
get is a diagnosis of which problem is in the way and a path with three steps.
The results depend on doing the steps.

**I think I have all three problems.** Most people feel that. In practice one
is costing you most right now, and fixing it changes the other two. The mapping
call is where we pick.

**I don't have an audience yet.** Then you are not in Red 2 whatever it feels
like. Start with Path 1: positioning first, then the content ecosystem, then
the demand loop.

**I already sell fine but I'm exhausted.** That's Red 3 wearing a Red 1 costume:
you're launching to keep revenue up. The fix is one signature system, not more
content.

**What does it cost / how long does it take?** Ask on the mapping call. The
guide doesn't quote prices or timelines.
